IDENTITY SHEET 1 / 3 IDEA, NAME, LOGO

CONCEPT

The Lift

Real-world assets have weight: land, buildings, funds held on paper. Off Paper is the show about lifting them onto the chain. Every piece is built on one horizon. Below it lies the ground, the real world. Above it, rings lift off the line and turn to face you: the tokens. Running to it, rails: the chain.

Primary lockup. "Off Paper" (Montserrat Bold, Mint) stands on the horizon rule; "- Powered by Tokeniser" (Medium, Light Mint) and the Tokeniser logo sit beneath it, the logo aligned to the end of the rule. Used on the cover and the end of the opening.

Compact lockup. The full name on one line in two weights, with the logo at the far edge. Used on thumbnails and small spaces.

THE NAME

Always written exactly Off Paper - Powered by Tokeniser: the ordinary hyphen-minus, a single space on each side, and these capitals. Never an en or em dash, extra punctuation, words run together or all caps. The name lives in one constant, SHOW_NAME in src/brand.js, and the automated checks fail on any other form.

IDENTITY SHEET 2 / 3 COLOUR AND TYPE

COLOUR: THE TOKENISER PALETTE, NOTHING ELSE

Share of a typical frame: Slate ground and Blue sky carry the show; Mint is the signal (name, rings, horizon); Light Mint for titles; Teal and Light Blue for the ground lines and rails. Orange is in the palette but is never used in show art: on a finance show it would look like a warning or a falling price. Light and haze are one palette colour laid over another at lower opacity.

IDENTITY SHEET 3 / 3 DEVICES, PHOTOS, MOTION

GRAPHIC DEVICES: 70 PERCENT OPACITY ON SOLID COLOUR, 60 PERCENT ON PHOTOS

Horizon

One line across every layout: the real world below, tokens above. It is also the rule in the show name.

Ground lines

Teal ridgelines: land, buildings, assets with weight. Near lines rise and fall; far lines flatten.

Rings

Mint tokens lifting off the horizon and turning to face you, heavier on the near edge like a coin's rim.

Rails

The chain: the ground lines made straight, Light Blue rails running to the horizon.

Light

Dawn along the horizon, glints on rings, pulses on rails, drifting motes. Light is never a device and never another colour.

PHOTOS

Guests sit in the coin: a Light Blue backdrop (cut-outs look best), a 30 percent Slate overlay on the photo, the rim at 70 percent on colour and the orbit at 60 percent where it crosses the photo. The maker applies all of it.

MOTION: THE OPENING, 16 SECONDS

Unhurried, precise easing and a single unbroken camera move. Music without voice; the name and logo are the only text. The last frames are solid Slate, ready for a hard cut.

APPLICATIONS

Cover 3000 x 3000, the 9:16 opening and a 16:9 thumbnail: one horizon, one name, one set of rings.
