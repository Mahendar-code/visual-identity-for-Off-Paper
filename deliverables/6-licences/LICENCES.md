## The stand-in guest photos

Off Paper has not recorded an episode yet, so the brief asks for example thumbnails that borrow titles and guests from earlier Redbelly Insights episodes. Those guests' own headshots were not used, because no copy with a licence allowing commercial reuse could be found. Using photos whose permission can be proven avoids the question entirely, so the examples use three **CC0 stock portraits** from Nappy in place of each guest's real headshot. **The people in these photos are not the guests named.** For real episodes, get the headshot from the guest and their written OK; [`guest-photo-release.md`](guest-photo-release.md) is a ready-made request.

All three were downloaded on 22 October 2026. [`src/photos/photos.json`](../../src/photos/photos.json) holds the same record as data. Check L01 in `tools/verify.py` re-hashes each file and confirms that its page capture shows the public domain mark.

**Nappy's terms.** The licence page, saved as [`proof/nappy-license.png`](proof/nappy-license.png) and [`proof/nappy-license.txt`](proof/nappy-license.txt), states: "All photos posted on Nappy are licensed under the Creative Commons Zero (CC0) license so feel free to do your thing. That means you can download these photos, modify them, share them, distribute them, or use them for whatever you want for free." It covers "personal and commercial projects" and allows changes to the photos. Under "More info", each photo page shows the "public domain" mark; see the `proof/photo-*.png` captures. The CC0 1.0 legal code is at https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

**Nappy's three restrictions, and how they are respected here.**

| Nappy restriction | What this project does |
|---|---|
| Do not repost the photos on other stock sites | The photos appear only inside the example thumbnails, the maker's examples and the identity sheet. |
| Do not resell the photos without significant changes | Nothing is sold. Each photo has its background removed, is placed in a coin under a 30 percent Slate wash, and is combined with type and graphics. |
| Do not use the photos to demean their subjects | Each person is shown as a respected podcast guest. |

**What was done to the photos.** The background was removed with rembg (MIT licence) using the IS-Net general-use model (Apache 2.0); see [`tools/cutout.py`](../../tools/cutout.py). Each photo was then scaled, cropped to the coin and given the Tokeniser 30 percent Slate wash. CC0 permits all of these changes.

**Recapturing the proof.** `node tools/licence-proof.mjs` opens the licence page and each photo page in a browser, expands "More info", and saves a fresh, dated screenshot and text capture.
