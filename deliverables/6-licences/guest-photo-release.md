# Guest photo release (for real episodes)

Use this when booking a guest, so every Off Paper thumbnail uses a photo the show is allowed to publish. Send it with the booking confirmation and keep the reply with the episode files.

---

**Off Paper - Powered by Tokeniser: photo for your episode**

Thank you for joining us. Please reply with:

1. A recent headshot, at least 1000 pixels on its shorter side. A photo with a plain background works best, and a cut-out (transparent PNG) is ideal.
2. The name of the photographer, if the photo needs a credit in the episode description.
3. Your confirmation of the following:

> I own this photo or have the right to share it, and I give Tokeniser and Redbelly permission to use it, cropped and colour-treated in the show's style, in the episode thumbnail, artwork and promotion of my Off Paper episode on YouTube, podcast platforms, social media and the Tokeniser and Redbelly websites.

Your name and role as you would like them to appear: ______________________

---

**Producer checklist**

- [ ] Written confirmation received and saved with the episode files
- [ ] Photographer credit, if any, added to the episode description (never on the thumbnail itself)
- [ ] Thumbnail made with the maker (`maker/`) and checked in its phone preview
